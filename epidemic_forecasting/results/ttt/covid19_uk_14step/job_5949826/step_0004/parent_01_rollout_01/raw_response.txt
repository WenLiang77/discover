### Explanation:
1. **Imputation**: We use `SimpleImputer` to handle missing values (zeros) in the training data.
2. **Seasonality Detection**: We assume a weekly seasonality (period of 7 days). If the data length is less than twice the seasonality period, we default to a constant seasonal component.
3. **SARIMAX Model**: We fit a SARIMAX model on the combined trend and seasonal data to capture both temporal dependencies and seasonality.
4. **Exponential Smoothing Model**: We also fit an exponential smoothing model directly on the original data to capture local trends.
5. **Combination of Forecasts**: We combine the forecasts from both models, preferring the SARIMAX model where available.
6. **Sanitization**: Negative forecasts are clipped to zero using `np.maximum`.

This approach aims to balance between capturing both global trends and regional-specific patterns, while handling potential issues like missing data and non-stationarity.
